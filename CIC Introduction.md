
# CIC Achievements

Acwa中国创新中心聚焦于新能源、海水淡化和氢能的技术研发。

在技术研发方面，已完成钢边框光伏组件项目，并通过交付验收。本项目联合通威实施，产品完成防腐、跟踪支架适配验证，有限元仿真和实物载荷测试，具备规模化部署条件。目前已实现 600MW 装机落地，累计节约成本 150 万美元，投资回报 16.7 倍。

配套的PV实验室具备端到端全流程测试能力，可支撑研发测试、FAT 工厂验收、可靠性验证、年度 SAT 现场验收、TDD 测试驱动开发，覆盖电气、衰减、机械、环境及现场无人机巡检等各类测试项目。

知识产权方面，累计申请 2 项发明专利（已公开）、6 项实用新型专利（实审中）及 1 项软件著作权（已授权），覆盖PV故障监测与数字化运维、组件机械载荷 / 绝缘 / 清洁测试、数据处理等方向。

ACWA China Innovation Center focuses on technology R&D for new energy, seawater desalination and hydrogen energy.

In terms of new technology development, the steel-frame PV module project has been completed and passed delivery acceptance. Implemented in partnership with Tongwei, the product has undergone anti-corrosion verification, tracker compatibility verification, FEA simulation and physical load testing, and is ready for large-scale deployment. It has achieved 600 MW of installed capacity, delivering a total cost saving of USD 1.5 million with a return multiple of 16.7x.

The supporting PV laboratory features end-to-end full-range testing capabilities. It supports R&D testing, FAT, reliability verification, annual SAT and TDD, covering electrical performance, degradation, mechanical performance, environmental adaptability, on-site drone inspection and other test categories.

On intellectual property, we have filed 2 invention patents (published), 6 utility model patents (under substantive examination) and obtained 1 software copyright (granted). These IP assets cover PV fault monitoring and digital O&M, module mechanical load / insulation / cleaning tests, data processing and related areas.
